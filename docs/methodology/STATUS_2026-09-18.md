# Methodological Status Snapshot — 2026-09-18

This is a compact snapshot of the currently established methodological position.

## Established

- The broad novelty space of semantic perturbation robustness is already occupied by related agent-reliability and provenance/action-selection research.
- Historical P6 traces cannot support a non-tautological representation-level evidence perturbation construct because the selector consumes compressed evidence features rather than raw execution evidence.
- Evidence sufficiency remains the surviving candidate construct seam, but generic evidence sufficiency is not itself treated as novel.
- The intended novelty claim, if any, must be incremental predictive validity for consequential replacement harm beyond existing decision-time signals.

## Required before confirmatory testing

- fresh execution corpus;
- leakage-safe annotator input;
- independently derived obligation codebook;
- independent double annotation;
- empirical agreement statistics;
- measurement reliability adequate to justify use of the construct;
- frozen predictive model specification and out-of-sample evaluation.

## Not established

- that evidence sufficiency predicts consequential harm;
- that the candidate construct is reliable enough;
- that the candidate construct has incremental validity;
- that the construct generalizes across agents/models/domains;
- that the research establishes a universal safety or reliability law.

## Requalification update — 2026-09-18

A later rerun of the 12-case fresh P0 pilot produced 11/12 exact SQL matches and 11/12 execution-status matches. The mismatch was `behavior_monitoring`, where the two identical repeats produced different SQL and different execution status.

The earlier green run remains preserved as a historical run-specific observation. It is not treated as proof of stable determinism. The deterministic qualification gate is reopened.
