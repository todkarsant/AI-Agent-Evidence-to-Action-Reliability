# C.4.2.4-A.3 — Literature Collision Refresh

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Purpose:** test whether the W1-W7 observable-evidence-witness construct or the exact incremental-validity claim is already established.

## Current literature inspected

1. **Auditing Provenance Sensitivity in LLM Agent Action Selection** — Junchi Liao, arXiv:2607.20827 (2026). It studies source-authority sensitivity and controlled evidence degradation in agent action selection. This is adjacent but targets provenance/authorization sensitivity, not an observable result-witness score predicting replacement harm.
2. **Do LLMs Know When Evidence Is Insufficient? An Evidence Sufficiency Benchmark for Answer-Abstention Calibration in Retrieval-Augmented Generation** — Hantian Zhang, Wentai Wu, Computers, Materials & Continua 89(1), 2026. It evaluates evidence sufficiency for abstention calibration, not W1-W7 result-observable witnesses as an incremental predictor of consequential replacement harm.
3. **S2G-RAG: Structured Sufficiency and Gap Judging for Iterative Retrieval-Augmented QA** — ACL 2026. It operationalizes sufficiency/gap judgments for retrieval trajectories, which is broader evidence sufficiency rather than the present witness construct.
4. **Verify Before You Commit: Towards Faithful Reasoning in LLM Agents via Self-Auditing** — ACL 2026. It concerns evidential/logical verification of agent reasoning, not the present observable-witness measurement and incremental harm-prediction estimand.
5. **From Evidence to Action: A Systematic Study of External Knowledge Grounding in LLM-based Agents** — Jie Wu, SSRN, August 2026. This is a working-paper/systematic-review stream on grounding and runtime verification; it does not establish the present construct or predictive-validity claim.
6. **From Agent Traces to Trust: Evidence Tracing and Execution Provenance in LLM Agents** — arXiv:2606.04990 (2026). It frames evidence tracing/provenance as an auditability problem, distinct from observable witness scoring.

## Attack conclusion

The refresh confirms substantial conceptual collision with generic reliability, evidence sufficiency, grounding, provenance, and trace-audit research. Those broad claims are therefore not available as novelty claims for P2-C1.2.

I did **not** find a verified source in this refresh that establishes the narrower empirical estimand: an independently human-measured, decision-time observable evidence-witness score that adds out-of-sample predictive information about consequential incumbent-replacement harm beyond a frozen baseline of execution/row/column signals.

This is a bounded literature-search conclusion, not a proof that no prior work exists. The claim must remain narrow and should be rechecked before journal submission.
