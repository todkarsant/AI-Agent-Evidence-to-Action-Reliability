# C4.2.4-A — Human Annotation Completion Checklist

Use this checklist independently for each rater.

## Before starting

- [ ] I have received only my assigned frozen packet.
- [ ] I have the frozen W1-W7 codebook.
- [ ] I have the response schema.
- [ ] I do not have access to another rater's labels.
- [ ] I will not inspect SQL, reference answers, correctness, intervention, or downstream outcomes.

## For every case

- [ ] I read the natural-language question.
- [ ] I read the returned columns and rows.
- [ ] I read the schema only as needed to interpret the displayed evidence.
- [ ] I separately considered applicability and witness presence.
- [ ] I used NO_USABLE_EVIDENCE without converting it to witness zeros when execution failed.
- [ ] I recorded UNCLEAR when applicability is genuinely unresolved rather than silently choosing an interpretation.
- [ ] My notes describe observable evidence, not hidden correctness.

## Before submission

- [ ] All 12 case IDs are present exactly once.
- [ ] Every case has W1-W7.
- [ ] Every successful case has valid applicability/witness combinations.
- [ ] Every NO_USABLE_EVIDENCE case has null witnesses.
- [ ] I have not seen or discussed the other rater's labels.
- [ ] I have not modified another rater's file.
- [ ] I have preserved my original raw response file.

## After both raters finish

The research owner, not the raters jointly, performs the mechanical validation and reliability analysis. Raw ratings must remain immutable.
